# Artificial Intelligence – Riwi

This repository contains exercises, practical activities, and learning materials developed during the Artificial Intelligence training sessions at Riwi.

The main objective is to explore fundamental AI concepts and develop practical skills through hands-on exercises.

## Project Structure

The repository is organized by training sessions:

```text
IA/
│
├── sesion-01/
│   └── Exercises and activities from session 01
│
├── sesion-02/
│   └── Exercises and activities from session 02
│
└── README.md
```

### Learning Objectives
Understand fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence.
Explore practical applications of AI.
Develop problem-solving skills through programming exercises.
Strengthen logical thinking and computational skills.
Apply concepts learned during training sessions.
